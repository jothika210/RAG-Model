# HR-203 — Sick Leave Addendum (EMEA)

Region: EMEA
Effective Date: 2026-01-01
Policy ID: HR-203

## 1. Purpose

This addendum supplements the base Employee Leave Handbook for employees working in the EMEA region (UK, Germany, France, UAE). It sets out sick leave entitlement, certification requirements, and interaction with statutory sick pay schemes.

### 1.1 Scope

Applies to all employees on EMEA payroll, including those on fixed-term contracts of 6 months or longer.

## 2. Entitlement

### 2.1 Standard Entitlement

Employees are entitled to 10 days of company-paid sick leave per calendar year at full salary, in addition to any statutory sick pay they may separately be entitled to under local law.

### 2.2 Extended Illness

For illness extending beyond 10 days, employees move to statutory sick pay (or local equivalent) as administered by local payroll, topped up per Section 2.3.

### 2.3 Company Top-Up

The company tops up statutory sick pay to 75% of base salary for a further 20 working days, subject to a doctor's certificate being on file per Section 3.

## 3. Certification Table

| Absence Duration | Certification Required | Notice to Manager |
|---|---|---|
| 1 day | Self-certification | Same day |
| 2–3 days | Self-certification | Same day |
| 4–7 days | Doctor's note | Within 24 hours |
| 8+ days | Doctor's note + People Ops review | Within 24 hours |

## 4. Carry-Over

### 4.1 No Carry-Over

Sick leave does not carry over between calendar years and has no cash-out value on termination. Unused sick days are forfeited on December 31st with no exceptions.
