# HR-201 — Annual Leave Addendum (APAC)

Region: APAC
Effective Date: 2026-01-01
Policy ID: HR-201

## 1. Purpose

This addendum supplements the base Employee Leave Handbook for employees working in the APAC region (Singapore, India, Australia, Japan). It sets out annual leave accrual, eligibility, and the carry-over process for the 2026 leave year.

### 1.1 Scope

This addendum applies to all full-time and part-time employees on APAC payroll. It does not apply to contractors engaged through third-party agencies, who are covered under their agency agreements instead.

### 1.2 Definitions

"Continuous service" means unbroken employment with the company from the employee's confirmed start date, excluding any period of unpaid leave exceeding 30 consecutive days. A break in continuous service resets accrual eligibility to zero. "Probationary employee" means an employee within their first 90 days of employment who has not yet received written confirmation of permanent status.

## 2. Accrual

### 2.1 Standard Accrual Rate

Full-time employees accrue annual leave at a rate of 1.75 days per completed month of continuous service, capped at 21 days per calendar year.

### 2.2 Part-Time Accrual

Part-time employees accrue annual leave on a pro-rata basis calculated as (contracted weekly hours / 40) × 1.75 days per completed month.

## 3. Eligibility Table

| Employment Status | Minimum Continuous Service | Annual Entitlement | Carry-Over Cap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Probationary | 0 months | 0 days (accrues, not usable) | Not applicable |
| Full-time confirmed | 3 months | 21 days | 5 days |
| Part-time confirmed | 3 months | Pro-rata of 21 days | 3 days |
| Fixed-term contract (< 12 months) | 3 months | 14 days | 0 days |

### 3.1 Notes on the Table

Probationary employees accrue leave from day one but may not take it until their probationary period ends and continuous service is confirmed in writing by People Ops. Unused accrual during probation is credited in full once confirmation occurs, subject to the standard carry-over cap in the row above.

## 4. Carry-Over

### 4.1 General Rule

Unused annual leave up to the carry-over cap specified in Section 3 may be carried into the following leave year. Any balance above the cap is forfeited on December 31st unless a regional exception under Section 4.2 applies.

### 4.2 Regional Exception — Singapore

Employees based in Singapore may carry over up to 7 days (not the standard 5-day cap) due to statutory minimum requirements under the Singapore Employment Act. This exception applies only to full-time confirmed employees; probationary and part-time employees remain subject to the caps in Section 3.
