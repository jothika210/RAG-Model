# HR-211 — Leave Carry-Over Cap Addendum (AMER)

Region: AMER
Effective Date: 2026-02-01
Policy ID: HR-211

## 1. Purpose

This addendum sets out annual leave carry-over caps for the AMER region, mirroring the structure of HR-207 (APAC) but with figures specific to AMER statutory requirements.

### 1.1 Definitions

"Continuous service" has the same meaning as defined in HR-201 Section 1.2, applied here to AMER-based employees under the AMER base leave entitlement (issued separately from HR-201, which is APAC-specific; the definition of continuous service is company-wide and not regional).

## 2. Revised Carry-Over Table

| Employment Status | Region | Carry-Over Cap | Effective |
|---|---|---|---|
| Full-time confirmed | United States | 5 days | 2026-02-01 |
| Full-time confirmed | Canada | 10 days | 2026-02-01 |
| Full-time confirmed | Mexico | 6 days | 2026-02-01 |
| Full-time confirmed | Brazil | 30 days (statutory) | 2026-02-01 |

### 2.1 Note on Brazil

Brazil's 30-day carry-over cap reflects statutory annual leave (férias) requirements and is not a company discretionary policy; it cannot be reduced by future addenda without legal review.

## 3. Section 4.2 — Probationary Employee Carry-Over Cap

### 4.2 Probationary Carry-Over Cap

A probationary employee in AMER who is confirmed to permanent status partway through the leave year is subject to a carry-over cap of 3 days for that transition year only, regardless of country. From the following leave year onward, the employee is subject to the standard country cap in Section 2.

## 4. Interaction with Parental Leave

Carry-over caps in this addendum do not apply to parental leave under HR-205, which is a per-event entitlement with no carry-over provision as stated in HR-205 Section 4.
