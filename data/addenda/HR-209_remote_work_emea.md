# HR-209 — Remote Work Leave Interaction Addendum (EMEA)

Region: EMEA
Effective Date: 2026-01-15
Policy ID: HR-209

## 1. Purpose

This addendum clarifies how annual and sick leave under HR-201 and HR-203 apply to employees approved for fully remote or hybrid work arrangements within EMEA, and sets out the public holiday interaction rule.

### 1.1 Scope

Applies to EMEA employees with an approved remote work agreement on file with People Ops.

## 2. Public Holiday Interaction

### 2.1 Home Country Holidays

Remote employees observe the public holidays of their registered home country, not the office location of their assigned manager or team.

### 2.2 Leave Deduction Rule

If a scheduled annual leave day coincides with a public holiday in the employee's registered home country, the day is credited back to the employee's leave balance and does not count against their annual entitlement under HR-201 Section 2.

## 3. Equipment and Leave-Adjacent Table

| Arrangement Type | Home Country Holidays Apply | Leave Balance Deduction on Holiday Overlap |
|---|---|---|
| Fully remote | Yes | Credited back (no deduction) |
| Hybrid (2+ office days/week) | Yes | Credited back (no deduction) |
| Temporary remote (<90 days) | No (office location applies) | Standard deduction applies |

## 4. Carry-Over

This addendum does not modify carry-over caps; employees remain subject to HR-201 Section 4 and, where applicable, HR-207 for APAC-based remote arrangements.
