# HR-207 — Leave Carry-Over Cap Addendum (APAC)

Region: APAC
Effective Date: 2026-02-01
Policy ID: HR-207

## 1. Purpose

This addendum clarifies annual leave carry-over caps for the APAC region where they differ from, or need further precision beyond, HR-201. Where HR-207 and HR-201 conflict on carry-over specifically, HR-207 (the more recent and more specific addendum) governs.

### 1.1 Definitions

"Continuous service" carries the same meaning defined in HR-201 Section 1.2: unbroken employment from the confirmed start date, excluding unpaid leave over 30 consecutive days. This definition is incorporated by reference and is not restated in the eligibility table below.

## 2. Background

Following a review of regional statutory minimums, People Ops is standardizing carry-over caps across APAC sub-regions for the 2026 leave year, with one specific adjustment for probationary employees clarified in Section 4.

## 3. Revised Carry-Over Table

| Employment Status | Region | Carry-Over Cap | Effective |
|---|---|---|---|
| Full-time confirmed | Singapore | 7 days | 2026-02-01 |
| Full-time confirmed | India | 10 days | 2026-02-01 |
| Full-time confirmed | Australia | 5 days | 2026-02-01 |
| Full-time confirmed | Japan | 5 days | 2026-02-01 |

## 4. Section 4.2 — Probationary Employee Carry-Over Cap

### 4.2 Probationary Carry-Over Cap

Notwithstanding Section 3, a probationary employee (as defined in HR-201 Section 1.2) who is confirmed to permanent status partway through the leave year is subject to a carry-over cap of 2 days for that transition year only, regardless of sub-region. This 2-day cap applies only to leave accrued during the probationary period itself; leave accrued after confirmation follows the standard caps in Section 3. From the following leave year onward, the employee is subject to the standard regional cap in Section 3.

## 5. Interaction with HR-201

Sections 1 through 2 and Section 4.1 of HR-201 remain in full effect. Only the carry-over cap figures are superseded, as set out in Section 3 and Section 4.2 above.
